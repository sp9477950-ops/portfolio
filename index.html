Hi, I'm Sahil 👋

I turn plain product shots into catalog-grade visuals

An AI Image Generation Engineer specialising in VTON, fabric re-texturing and high-precision product retouching — building diffusion pipelines that keep geometry exact while reimagining everything around it.

See the transformations → Work with me

I specialize in building AI systems that treat generation as engineering, not guesswork. My fabric re-texturing and VTON work swaps materials onto garment wireframes while preserving 100% of the source's physical shape, outline, neckline depth, and edge geometry.

On the retouching side, I drive ghost-mannequin pipelines that generate seamless hollow interiors and erase wrinkles while keeping volumetric roundness intact. For home furnishings, I project patterns onto draped and tucked surfaces with zero design bleeding.

Every pipeline is governed by hard constraints — scale lock, geometry lock, negative prompts — so retail and fashion catalogs ship clean, consistent, production-ready assets.
